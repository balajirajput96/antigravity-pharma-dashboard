# Reel 0003 — Retrieval Practice: Production Blueprint

**Status:** Evidence and creative-preproduction complete; media generation has not started.  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, target duration 60 seconds, Devanagari Hindi captions.  
**Creative brief confirmed by owner:** Warm neutral female Hindi narration; cinematic scientific 2D/3D learning-and-memory visuals; subtle ambient bed; no medical or educational guarantee claims.

## Evidence Boundary

> **Scientific evidence:** Practice testing/retrieval practice was more beneficial for learning than restudying and other comparison conditions in the Adesope et al. meta-analysis. Effects were moderated by test, participant, outcome, and methodological characteristics.[1]

> **Scientific evidence:** Classroom research reviewed by Agarwal et al. reported benefits across many of the studied implementations, but the literature includes varied implementations and limited representation outside WEIRD settings.[2]

> **Not claimed:** This Reel does not promise a universal result, a percentage gain, a particular exam score, treatment, memory enhancement, or benefit for every learner.

## Production Requirements

- Generate visual clips without embedded words, logos, source names, statistics, watermark-like elements, or captions.
- Generate/record Hindi narration from the approved script only; maintain a measured, warm, neutral explanatory delivery.
- Build captions separately in Devanagari with a single high-contrast safe band and verify final cue ends at or before actual video duration.
- Preserve all generated candidates, rejected versions, source citations, narration track, captions, QA report, and metadata locally until the final private Drive package is exact-name verified.
- Keep the final package private. No public upload, social-post copy, or automatic media schedule is in scope.
