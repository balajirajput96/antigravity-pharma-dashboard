# Reel 0004 Production Blueprint — Spacing Is Not a Magic Calendar

## Scope and Claim Control

**Format:** Private, captioned, 9:16 Hindi educational reel, target runtime approximately 55–60 seconds.

**Evidence status:** The core claim is **strong evidence, qualified**: distributing learning episodes across time can support later retention better than immediately massed repetition in many studied contexts. The useful interval is not a fixed rule; it depends in part on the later retention interval and context.[1] A child science-learning study is included only as a bounded illustration, not as a general promise.[2]

> **Do not claim:** a universal 24-hour schedule, an exact mark increase, a cure for forgetfulness, a brain-change guarantee, or that every learner should use expanding intervals. The video may present spacing as a learning-planning experiment, not treatment or certainty.[1] [3]

## Visual Direction

The visual route is **caption-free procedural motion graphics plus simple editorial illustration**, not AI-generated medical/brain imagery. The final editor will burn in captions from a separate Hindi SRT. Keep the lower 22% of each scene unobstructed for captions.

| Segment | Visual | Safety / accessibility rule |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Five study cards stack into one crowded night, then separate across a calm horizontal timeline. | Avoid a score meter or “wrong/right” implication. |
| Evidence | Repeating concept cards land on spaced calendar nodes. | Use neutral labels such as “session” and “later”, not exact schedules. |
| No magic gap | A calendar dial shows multiple possible intervals without naming a universal number. | No 24-hour visual prescription. |
| Review finding | A curved distance-to-later-test path shifts across time. | Show “context matters” icon, not brain scans. |
| Child-study limitation | Small classroom-style concept cards with an on-screen “specific study” label. | Do not depict identifiable children or claim universal classroom results. |
| Practical experiment | Personal planning sheet with three blank review dates and a reflection marker. | Do not claim the plan guarantees memory. |
| Takeaway | The timeline resolves into a calm learning-plan card with references. | Keep full source card readable for at least three seconds. |
